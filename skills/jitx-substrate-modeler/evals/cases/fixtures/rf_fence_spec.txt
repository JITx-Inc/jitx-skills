RF fencing requirements for RFE-6L-FENCE (synthetic example)

Fab report: rf_PC-6L_Fab-Stackup_RevA.csv (Pellingham Circuits, fictional).
Board outline: 30.0 x 20.0 mm rectangle centered on the origin, square corners.
Fence vias, the cavity ring and the antipad ring belong to the GND net.

1. 50 ohm grounded coplanar line on L1 (IMPEDANCE row SE-50)
   - Fence both sides of the line with UV-1 (L1 to L2), tied to the L2 ground plane.
   - Fence via pitch 0.50 mm, one row on each side.
   - The first row's via centers sit 0.40 mm out from the trace edge.
   - Void the L2 copper under the line, 0.60 mm wide.

2. 100 ohm differential pair on L1 and L6 (IMPEDANCE row DIFF-100)
   - Fence the pair on each layer with the microvia that lands on that layer's adjacent ground:
     UV-1 to L2 on L1, UV-2 to L5 on L6.
   - Fence via pitch 0.50 mm, one row on each side.
   - The first row's via centers sit 0.60 mm out from the outer trace edge.

3. RF cavity on L1
   - Cavity outline: a 4.0 x 3.0 mm rectangle centered at (5.0, 3.0) mm.
   - Ring the outline with UV-1 fence vias landing on L2: pitch 0.50 mm, one row,
     via centers 0.30 mm outside the outline.
   - No copper on L1 inside the outline.

4. 50 ohm stripline on L4 (IMPEDANCE row SL-50)
   - Fence both sides of the line with TH-1 (L1 to L6), tied to the L3 and L5 ground planes.
   - Fence via pitch 1.00 mm, one row on each side.
   - The first row's via centers sit 0.80 mm out from the trace edge.
   - The line drops from L1 to L4 through a TH-1 signal via at (-8.0, -4.0) mm.
   - Antipad: a 1.10 mm diameter circle centered on that via, with no copper on L2, L3 or L5 inside it.
   - Ring the antipad with TH-1 return vias tied to the L2, L3 and L5 planes: pitch 1.00 mm, one row,
     via centers 0.60 mm outside the antipad.
